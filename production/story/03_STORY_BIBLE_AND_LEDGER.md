Fairness note (kept visible to teacher): the 20 s overrun vs 10 s overrun gives students a genuine unequal-treatment datum *without* proving motive. Who cut the mic (a hand, not shown) stays unresolved in the clip; in the full account it is the venue technician following the chair's timer - **to be decided and locked before scripting scene 3** (my default: technician Sam, acting on the chair's cue; Maya looked at the clock but did not give the cue - this must remain arguable, so the cue is ambiguous: a nod toward the technician that can read as a signal or as clock-checking).

## Characters (voice, want, flaw, fair argument)
- **MAYA** (40s, director): wants the space alive; flaw: announced the change badly and late; fair: "Six months of rent is not an abstraction."
- **LEON** (60s, volunteer): wants access kept; flaw: assumes motives without seeing the books; fair: "A free workshop that closes at weekday evenings isn't free for me."
- **TESS** (30s, videographer): wants accountability; flaw: headline outran evidence; fair: "I filmed what happened. The clip is real."
- **ADRIAN** (50s, training company): wants predictable room use; flaw: treats the schedule as a detail; fair: "I'm paying so the doors stay open; I can't pay for a room I can't use."

## Opening hook (Scene 1, ~60 s) - working script
LEON: "You can't call it a community space if the community can't use it." -> Maya glances at wall clock -> hand at the sound desk -> audio cut (sound drops to room tone) -> Tess's phone, screen shows red record dot -> phone feed: the 18-s clip with caption card "WORKSHOP DIRECTOR SILENCES VOLUNTEER FOR BIG-MONEY DEAL" -> share counter climbs -> close-ups: Maya (jaw tight), Leon (stunned, then steady), Tess (lowered phone, unsure), Adrian (looks at his notes). **GATE 0:** "What do you think happened? What can you actually prove?"

## Class flow & gates
G0 interpretation (4 min) -> G1 positions (6) -> Evidence reveal 1: documents + timeline (8, pairs) -> **Decision 1 (viral post, A-D)** (10) -> consequences/new evidence (7) -> role negotiation (8) -> **Decision 2 (future, A-D)** (7) -> ending module + comparison (7) -> exit response (3). Player never auto-advances at a gate.

## Branch map (manageable)
Decision 1 (A/B/C/D) sets a state `trust` in {context-added, removed, uncertainty-label, separate-correction}. Decision 2 (A/B/C/D) selects 1 of **4 ending clips**. Early choice changes **only** a short 8-10 s "bridge line" and captions (state-based), not the locked facts: 4 x 4 = 16 combos documented, **4 ending videos + 4 bridge variants** to render.

## Decision 1 trade-offs (no "right" answer)
| Opt | Protects | Costs / risk | Implementation problem |
|---|---|---|---|
| A context prominently | transparency, Leon's visibility | first impression persists | people share the bare clip |
| B temporary removal | Maya from harm while checking | looks like hiding; reduces transparency | copies already exist |
| C keep + uncertainty statement | honesty about what is known | Tess must edit her caption; reach limited | wording must be precise |
| D separate correction, linked | clean record | correction reaches fewer viewers | link may be ignored |

## Decision 2 trade-offs
| Opt | Gains | Costs |
|---|---|---|
| A accept $6,000 + published conditions | gap closed with $200 spare | 10 respondents lose access; 12 unknown; conditions may not be agreed |
| B reject + alternatives | access kept | success uncertain; 6-month gap unfunded |
| C $4,500 compromise | partial access | $1,300 gap remains |
| D 7-day extension | time to negotiate | offer may lapse |
