About the sound, IPA and your data

All music and sound effects are generated by code in your browser. Voices use your device's speech synthesis; they are not human recordings and sound different on each device.

IPA uses only the symbols on the Fluent English Sound Chart (American English). Each word was transcribed from that symbol set, but the words could not be looked up on Oxford Learner's Dictionaries when this lesson was built, so no Oxford verification is claimed. Sentence lines are assembled word by word from citation forms (articles use weak forms); they are not Oxford sentence transcriptions. Please check important words yourself.

No names, e-mail addresses or microphone data are collected. Settings, progress and scores stay in this browser. Exports happen only when you press a download button.
