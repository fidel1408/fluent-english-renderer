About the voices and IPA

Speech uses your device’s voices (Web Speech API). Quality varies: natural/neural voices (Edge, Chrome on Android/macOS) sound best; some browsers have none for Spanish or English.

IPA is General American in the style of the Oxford Learner’s (American) dictionary. It was hand-checked, but not verified against a live dictionary in this build.

The practice pause is silent and is not speech recognition: nothing is graded.
